Vinay Sharma
Founder of Mandvi Yoga, Hatha, Mantra, Ashtanga

Vinay is a RYT-200 hour Yoga teacher certified by Yoga Alliance USA. He began his career as a Meditation specialist and, realising his passion for Yoga, transitioned into a Yoga teacher, making it his lifelong profession.​

Vinay has completed his post-graduate diploma in Yoga, followed by a Master’s in Yogic Science & Yogic Consciousness from Bhopal University, Madhya Pradesh, India. With over 15 years of experience, he has been practicing Yoga since childhood, drawn to Yogic and karmic philosophies, which have led him to various spiritual locations.

Having traveled extensively across major palaces in India, Vinay has been teaching Yoga and Meditation, including Thai Yoga, Therapeutic Yoga, Prenatal-post Natal, Child Yoga, Kriya Yoga, Stress Management, Pranayama, and Meditation courses.

Neeraj (Yogi Raj)
Yoga Scholar, Hatha, Kriya

RYT-200h yoga teacher certified by Yoga Alliance and one of the senior yoga trainers at Sant Retreats Wellness Centre, Neeraj started his career as a yoga trainer. He slowly realized his passion for yoga and made it his lifelong profession.

Neeraj completed his postgraduate diploma in Yogic Science from Uttarakhand Sanskrit University, Haridwar. He has been practicing yoga for more than 11 years. After completing his yoga education, he traveled to different parts of India, teaching yoga, therapeutics, weight management, pranayama, meditation, and kriya yoga.

Debbie West
Holistic Nutritionist, Hatha, Restorative

Debbie is a holistic wellness guide who discovered the healing power of yoga through her own health challenges. With extensive training in Sivananda Yoga Vedanta, Therapeutic Iyengar, and Functional Yin & Restorative practices, she brings a diverse approach to her teaching.

Having lived and volunteered in ashrams across different countries, Debbie humbly shares her profound connection with yoga. Her mission is to create a welcoming space for students of all levels, making the practice adaptable to everyone. Join Debbie on the mat for a transformative journey toward balance, peace of mind, and overall well-being.

Debbie is a certified holistic nutritionist with a focus on lifestyle transformation and metabolic health. With a deep understanding that true wellness stems from sustainable lifestyle changes rather than quick fixes, she guides her clients toward optimal health through nutrient-dense, ancestral dietary practices. Drawing from her own experience overcoming chronic health challenges, Debbie emphasizes a balanced approach that integrates proper nutrition, mindful living, and natural rhythms.
